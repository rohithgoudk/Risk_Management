Stackly started as an internal tool for a logistics company that lost a quarter's margin to a supplier failure no one saw coming. We rebuilt it for everyone else.

Our Position

We believe risk management fails when it lives in quarterly reports instead of daily operations. Stackly was built to move it into the room where decisions actually get made — continuous, visible, and owned by the people closest to the exposure.

2019

A supplier failure, and a spreadsheet that couldn't keep up

Founders Mara Ilić and Tom Okafor were running risk for a mid-size logistics operator when a single-supplier dependency they'd flagged too late cost the company a full quarter's margin.

2020

Stackly begins as an internal system

The first version of the risk matrix was built to track exposure across their own supply chain — not to sell, just to never be caught off guard again.

2022

Opened to the first outside customers

Word spread among peers in operations and finance. Stackly left the building it was built in and became a product.

2024

Cyber and compliance modules added

Customers asked for the same clarity applied to regulatory change and digital exposure. Two new categories joined the matrix.

2026

1,240+ organizations now run on Stackly

From single-site manufacturers to global financial institutions, all watching the same kind of signal, before it becomes a problem.
